## Recommendation

Given the prior desk rejections from clinical-epidemiology / trial journals, the strongest specialised fit is **Trials** under the **Methodology** article type. It explicitly accepts new computational methods, requires a reproducible data/code statement, has no restrictive word/figure limits, and the manuscript already contains the required Declarations section.

If APC budget is a concern, **F1000Research** ($1,758) or **PLOS ONE** ($2,477) are lower-cost alternatives, though they are megajournals with broader scope.

Avoid re-submitting immediately to **Contemporary Clinical Trials / CCTC / Clinical Trials** without a major reframing, as all have desk-rejected the same empirical framing.
