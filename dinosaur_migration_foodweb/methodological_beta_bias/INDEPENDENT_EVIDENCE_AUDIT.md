# Independent evidence audit — Allosaurus (limited review)

Purpose (per protocol): determine whether any *truly independent*
evidence contradicts the methodological conclusion — NOT to test or
rescue the migration hypothesis. Conducted as a bounded literature scan,
September 2026.

Classification scale: SUPPORTS exceptional mobility / CONSISTENT BUT
NON-DIAGNOSTIC / SUPPORTS generalism only / NO SUPPORT / CONTRADICTORY.

## Evidence classes reviewed

| evidence class | examples found | classification |
|---|---|---|
| Stable isotopes (δ¹⁸O, δ¹³C, δ⁴⁴Ca) | Hohensee et al. 2025 (GSA abstr. 407954): Nail Quarry *A. fragilis* shed teeth — ontogenetic niche partitioning; C/O/Ca isotope study of Morrison sauropods + *A. fragilis* (GSA 2022, abstr. 377333) | **CONSISTENT BUT NON-DIAGNOSTIC** — isotopes inform diet/niche, not movement; no serial-enamel δ¹⁸O migration transect exists for Allosaurus |
| Trackways | *Hispanosauropus* in Salt Wash Member (Lockley et al. 2015, Ichnos 10.1080/10420940.2015.1059335); ~250 trackways at ~50 Morrison localities across all exposed states | **CONSISTENT BUT NON-DIAGNOSTIC** — allosauroid attribution uncertain; track distributions show range, not movement mechanism |
| Direct diet evidence | Mygatt-Moore Quarry bite-mark survey (Drumheller et al. 2020, PLOS ONE 10.1371/journal.pone.0233115): 29% of specimens bear theropod marks, Allosaurus + Ceratosaurus striations, scavenging and cannibalism; Stegosaurus plate bite (Carpenter 2005); sauropod-carrion feasibility (Pahl & Rummel 2022) | **SUPPORTS generalism only** — broad prey spectrum incl. carrion; consistent with trophic generalism, unrelated to mobility |
| Ontogenetic spatial structure | Nail Quarry shed teeth across ontogenetic stages | **CONSISTENT BUT NON-DIAGNOSTIC** — local accumulation of age classes, not movement |
| Palaeobiogeographic studies | *A. fragilis* widespread in Morrison; *A. europaeus* reported from Lourinhã (Portugal) | **CONSISTENT BUT NON-DIAGNOSTIC** — continental-scale occurrence reflects lineage longevity/dispersal, not seasonal mobility |
| Direct migration tests | none found — no isotopic transect, no GPS-analogue evidence, no trackway directionality study demonstrating seasonal round-trips | **NO SUPPORT** |

## Conclusion

No independent evidence reviewed *contradicts* the methodological
conclusion, and none independently *supports* exceptional mobility. The
strongest positive evidence class (bite marks, isotopes) supports dietary
generalism rather than movement. This audit found nothing that requires
reopening the mobility hypothesis; equally, nothing was found that would
demonstrate it — the evidence base is dominated by trophic, not
locomotor, data.

**Bounded scan disclaimer:** this is a limited review, not a systematic
review; it should be expanded if the manuscript's Discussion cites
mobility-adjacent evidence.
