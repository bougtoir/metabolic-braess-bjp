# Ancient marine observation model

**Biological process**: marine trophic structure (primary consumers / grazers /
suspension feeders vs nektonic & benthic predators) over basins and
palaeogeographic units.

**Observation process**: sedimentary fossil collections — the collection is
a stratum/bed sample convolved with facies; marine connectivity means
assemblages are not discrete "localities"; transport (time-averaged shelly
beds) mixes communities.

**Preservation**: mineralization filter (aragonite/calcite dissolution),
stratigraphic condensation (centuries–Myr per bed), facies tracking by
taxa (environmental fidelity, not ecological dispersal).

**Corrections**: collection-intensity normalization, facies-stratified
turnover, monodominant-bed exclusion, grid/province pooling sensitivity.
Geographic bias toward museum-classic basins is scored in
`observation_model_matrix.csv`.
