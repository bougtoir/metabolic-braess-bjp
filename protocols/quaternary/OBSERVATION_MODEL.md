# Quaternary observation model

**Biological process**: Plio-Pleistocene–Holocene mammal food-web spatial
coupling across megafaunal extinction and deglaciation.

**Observation process**: PBDB Quaternary collections; finer dating than deep
time (radiocarbon for late Quaternary); strong geographic bias toward
caves/tar pits; megafauna extinction creates a compositional break mid-window
— event bins must be defined around it.

**Preservation**: cave/tar-pit accumulations = predator-modified assemblages
(guild ratio biased toward carnivore-accumulated prey); documented in adapter
as a first-class bias, not hidden in cleaning.

**Corrections**: dinosaur battery + fine-resolution ladder; extant-descendant
taxonomy is better resolved than Cenozoic — resolution check required.
