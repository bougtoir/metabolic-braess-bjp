# Nature go / no-go

**Classification: NO-GO**

Reasons:

- Primary directional hypothesis falsified (Morrison Δβ < 0, opposite to
  predicted Δβ > 0).
- The opposite signal was exploratory only and attributable to a single
  taxon (Allosaurus).
- Independent prospective replication in the Nemegt Formation failed
  (Δβ_full = +0.094, CI [−0.079, +0.300]; locked criteria, Outcome D).
- The dominant-predator mechanism did not replicate (removing Tarbosaurus
  did not attenuate Δβ toward zero; it moved it further positive).
- Therefore no basis currently exists for the proposed general
  cross-Mesozoic food-web claim.

No attempt is made to rescue Nature-level generality through additional
post hoc formations. Re-evaluation would require a separately preregistered
programme producing an unexpectedly general result (mechanism identified,
independent systems or modern analogues supporting it, implications beyond
Allosaurus).

Successor work lives in `allosaurus_spatial_ecology/` — an explicitly
**exploratory** project that does not inherit confirmatory status.
