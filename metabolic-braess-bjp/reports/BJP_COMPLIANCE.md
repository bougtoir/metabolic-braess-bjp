# BJP compliance checklist (verified against current author guidelines)

- Article type: Original Research — OK.
- Title: ≤200 characters — OK (~130).
- Abstract: structured (Background and Purpose / Experimental Approach /
  Key Results / Conclusion and Implications), 204 words (<250) — OK.
- Word limit: main text ~2,500 (<4,000 excl. abstract/Methods/refs/
  legends) — OK.
- Bullet-point summary: 5 statements ≤15 words each — OK.
- Keywords: 6 (3–7 range) — OK.
- References: 21 (<60), numbered by appearance — OK.
- Display items: 5 figures + 4 tables = 9 (≤10) — OK.
- Graphical abstract: required at first revision — NOT YET PREPARED
  (only needed at revision stage).
- CRediT authorship: submission/credit_authorship.md — OK.
- AI disclosure: included in submission docs — verify journal-specific
  wording at submission.
- Data/code availability: public repo + deterministic rebuild — OK.
- Experimental-design reporting (BJP/Curtis rubric): n/a items
  (computational study) documented as such.
