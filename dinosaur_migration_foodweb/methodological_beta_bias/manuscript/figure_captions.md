# Figure captions (Figures 1–5)

**Figure 1. Conceptual bias framework.** Observed guild-level
beta-diversity contrast decomposed as
Δβ_obs = Δβ_eco + B_γ + B_D + B_T + B_L + B_S + ε: an ecological
contrast plus structural terms for gamma-diversity imbalance (B_γ),
dominant-taxon structure (B_D), temporal averaging (B_T), taxonomic
lumping (B_L) and sampling asymmetry (B_S). Terms are not assumed
additive; interactions are evaluated factorially (Fig. 4). To be
redrawn as a schematic; current placeholder narrative panel in
figures/fig1_narrative.png.

**Figure 2. Simulation — gamma diversity and dominance.**
(a) Apparent predator–herbivore contrast Δβ_obs under Δβ_true = 0 as
predator pool size varies (herbivore pool fixed at 26): the four-taxon
pool marks the representative Morrison-like asymmetry, the two-taxon
pool the most extreme tested condition; error bars are 95% replicate
intervals (200 replicates per cell). (b) Δβ_obs as one predator taxon
is elevated to dominance p_D = 0–0.9; dashed line marks the
Allosaurus-like prevalence (~0.46). Throughout, negative Δβ indicates
apparent predator continuity (predators more spatially homogeneous
than herbivores).

**Figure 3. Simulation — taxonomic lumping and temporal averaging.**
(a) Δβ_obs when k geographically partitioned species are pooled into a
single genus (species-resolved vs genus-pooled); the lumping term
reaches +0.099 apparent continuity at k = 6. (b) Δβ_obs as temporal
resolution is coarsened from 8 thin slices to a single pooled bin,
erasing a genuine within-slice contrast.

**Figure 4. Factorial bias map.** Heatmap of Bias = Δβ_obs − Δβ_true
across predator gamma (2–26) × dominance p_D (0–0.9), one panel per
lumping level k ∈ {1, 3, 6}. Sign-reversal and false-difference
(|Δβ_obs| > 0.1) regions occupy the low-gamma, high-dominance,
high-lumping corner; biases are non-additive.

**Figure 5. Morrison empirical attenuation sequence.** Sequential
diagnostics on the primary specification (Jaccard, collection level):
naive genus-level Δβ = −0.550; points with error bars are observed
values with collection-bootstrap CIs; shaded bands are null-model
simulation intervals (gamma-matched and frequency-matched), a distinct
quantity from the observed CIs; Δβ after Allosaurus exclusion (−0.020)
and at species resolution (−0.322). Nemegt comparator (+0.094, CI
crossing zero) shown as an inset or Supplementary Figure.

*Supplementary figures:* sampling-asymmetry sweep (sim05), Nemegt
dominant-predator diagnostics, occurrence maps, convergence details.
