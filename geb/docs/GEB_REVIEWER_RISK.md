# GEB reviewer risk — pre-empted objections

| # | objection | best response | additional analysis needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Lagged abundance must be predictive by definition — demographic continuity is trivial." | That is precisely the point: abundance state dependence is expected and confirmed (+0.63 log-count, 40/41 CI>0), while occupancy "memory" — the version usually claimed — is unsupported. The contrast is the finding, not the autocorrelation itself. | No; sharpen framing. |
| 2 | "Route demeaning removes true biology — the 'static persistence' could itself be accumulated history." | Agreed and stated: the fixed effect absorbs any persistent route property including slowly-accumulated suitability. That ambiguity is why we test prospective prediction on top of demeaning: residual HG_D ≈ +0.006 bounds whatever genuine history remains. The paper claims the *predictive* signal is mostly static, not that routes have no history. | No. |
| 3 | "Annual resolution is too coarse for memory." | True for fine-grained individual memory; the manuscript's claim is about route-level distributional dynamics, where annual BBS is the standard temporal unit and 58-year coverage compensates for coarse resolution. | No; limitation + scope wording. |
| 4 | "Environmental covariates are incomplete." | Bounded wording throughout: "at matched modelled environment". Residual habitat heterogeneity is a limitation, and it biases *toward* apparent history dependence — so the near-zero occupancy result is conservative in the direction that matters. | No. |
| 5 | "BBS detectability / observer variation." | Observer covariates controlled (observer-controlled HG ≈ 0.009); survey semantics corrected (unsurveyed ≠ absent); imperfect detection acknowledged. | Partially: observer-*persistence* check for the abundance effect is the one optional analysis (GAP_AUDIT §10). |
| 6 | "Why call this 'history dependence' at all?" | Because the field does: lagged-state predictors in SDMs are routinely read as memory/hysteresis. The paper tests whether that reading survives explicit separation — it mostly doesn't for occupancy. | No. |
| 7 | "Generality beyond birds?" | Claimed only conceptually: the confound (lagged state ∝ persistent site quality) is taxon-general; the empirical demonstration is BBS. BBS is the largest long-run standardized vertebrate survey — a reasonable place to establish the point. | No. |
| 8 | "Static habitat quality is expected — trivial result." | Expected in principle, unquantified in practice: the 2×2 decomposition shows it accounts for ~95% of apparent history gain (0.30→0.006 forward). Quantifying the inflation across 116 species and showing it survives prospective validation is the contribution. | No. |
| 9 | "Abundance prior-state effect may be mathematically trivial (mechanical autocorrelation)." | The effect is estimated on route-demeaned abundance at matched environment with forward-chaining — mechanical autocorrelation is partially absorbed by the demean. Magnitude (+0.63) far exceeds the occupancy signal (0.000), so it is not a generic panel artifact. | The observer-persistence check (§5) also addresses this. |
| 10 | "Negative results are not sufficiently novel." | The paper has a positive result — the occupancy/abundance divergence across 116 species — plus a methodological warning with quantitative magnitude. Framed as "what lagged distributions actually measure", not "memory is false". | No. |
