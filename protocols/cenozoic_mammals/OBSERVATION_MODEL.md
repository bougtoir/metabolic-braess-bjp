# Cenozoic mammals observation model

**Biological process**: Miocene–Pliocene herbivore ranges vs carnivore
assemblage turnover in North America.

**Observation process**: PBDB fossil collections; no analogue of Morrison
bone beds but Cenozoic equivalents exist (e.g. Ashfall-style mass-death
beds, cave accumulations, mammal-pit traps). Collection intensity is much
higher and denser than the dinosaur record; taxonomy is finer (many
indeterminate dentitions).

**Preservation**: time averaging within collections is shorter than
formation-scale dinosaur bins but still present; small mammal sampling is
screen-wash dependent (collector bias toward micro-mammal sites).

**Guild mapping difference**: dinosaur guilds = three clades; mammal guilds
require an order list (herbivores) vs Carnivora (predators) with marine
families (pinnipeds) and omnivore ambiguity handled explicitly
(`indeterminate`/mixed-diet taxa excluded from matrices).

**Corrections**: identical five-correction battery as dinosaur; temporal
aggregation ladder added because Cenozoic temporal precision is higher.
