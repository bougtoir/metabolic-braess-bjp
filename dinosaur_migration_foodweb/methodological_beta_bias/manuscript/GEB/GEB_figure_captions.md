# GEB figure captions (main figures 1–6)

**Figure 1. Conceptual framework.** True guild turnover is filtered
through assemblage structure — regional pool size, dominance,
taxonomic and temporal aggregation, sampling asymmetry — before
Δβ_observed is measured; observed contrasts therefore need not equal
ecological contrasts. Schematic (to be drawn).

**Figure 2. Structural asymmetry creates false differentiation
(Δβ_true = 0).** (a) Δβ_obs vs small-guild regional pool size
(comparison guild = 26 taxa); four-taxon pool is the representative
case (−0.17) and the two-taxon pool the extreme tested condition
(−0.41); points are means over 200 replicates with 95% replicate
intervals. (b) Δβ_obs vs dominant-taxon prevalence p_D; negative
Δβ = apparent spatial continuity of the small/dominant-structured
guild. Frozen seed set 20260922.

**Figure 3. Two-dimensional robustness and distance-decay
distortion.** (a) Δβ_obs under pool asymmetry on a 40×40 lattice with
disc-shaped ranges (pool 4: −0.21; pool 2: −0.47; 100 replicates,
seed 20260924), replicating the 1-D pattern. (b) Mean pairwise Jaccard
dissimilarity vs pairwise geographic distance (β(d)) for small-pool
vs comparison guilds; fitted decay slopes ≈0.028 vs ≈0.006 — the
bias inflates the apparent rate of spatial turnover, not just its
mean. Dependent variable: pairwise Jaccard; distance in lattice
units; slope sign positive = increasing dissimilarity with distance.

**Figure 4. Taxonomic aggregation and temporal aggregation.**
(a) Pooling k geographically partitioned species into one genus adds
apparent continuity; B_lumping = β_species − β_genus (positive = more
continuity), up to +0.099 at k = 6. (b) Temporal pooling attenuates
observed guild contrasts toward zero (1-D −0.031 → −0.001; 2-D
confirmatory −0.045 → −0.018), a false-negative mechanism.

**Figure 5. Factorial bias map.** Bias = Δβ_obs − Δβ_true across
pool size × dominance, one panel per lumping level; sign-reversal and
false-difference regions occupy the low-gamma/high-dominance corner
(max |Bias| −0.91, 100% false-difference rate). The additive
decomposition is conceptual only; this map is the estimable summary.

**Figure 6. Empirical stress test (Morrison Formation).** Sequential
attenuation: naive Δβ = −0.550 (−0.630, −0.457); gamma- and
frequency-matched null intervals shown shaded (distinct from
bootstrap CIs); dominant-taxon exclusion Δβ = −0.020; species
resolution Δβ = −0.322; dominant-taxon extent residual −36.6 km
(z = −0.16). Inset: Nemegt comparator Δβ = +0.094 (−0.079, +0.300).
